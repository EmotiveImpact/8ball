# Four source projects → a stronger native 8BALL fixer workflow

## Owner verdict

**Keep the previous native specialist-coordination / bounded-rehearsal direction, but make it smaller and more auditable. Do not install these four projects into ENDSTATE.** The useful sequence is: preserve the objection, compare the explicit assumption, show the exact basis, and let the human decide. More agents, weighted agreement, simulation engines or dashboards are not the missing foundation.

## Six strongest transferable patterns

### 1. Preserve an objection and explain its disposition; do not manufacture consensus

**Observed:** Loomio [requires disagreement/block reasons in a consent template](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/config/poll_templates.yml#L236-L259) and [creates an explicitly authored outcome](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/services/outcome_service.rb#L31-L79). But [stance edits](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/services/stance_service.rb#L38-L83) do not always create a replacement, and [zero-score handling](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/services/poll_service.rb#L552-L574) can satisfy the template's zero-objection numerical test with no votes. That arithmetic is not proof of consent or a runtime-confirmed Loomio defect.

**Overlap and benefit:** build on Plan Review, Insights and course rationale. Link a contribution to a typed assumption/criterion/course reference, keep its original wording, and append the operator's explanation: revise, seek evidence, defer, or proceed while explicitly retaining the unresolved objection. Label nonresponse separately. An operator-authored archetype is a hypothetical perspective—not an authenticated specialist or witness.

**Limits/rights:** no vote weights, psychological profiles, majority-to-fact transitions or “block” label granting veto authority. Do not adopt Loomio's notifications/invitations or mutable-row heuristic. AGPL source/UI/template copying requires separate review; use original native expression.

**Recommendation: adapt native. Acceptance experiment:** twelve fictional review fixtures containing zero input, support, uncertainty, dissent and nonresponse. Require every original contribution and predecessor to survive an explained disposition/export; all stale or cross-case submissions fail; identical retries are idempotent. Compare the existing rubric-only workflow with the bounded contribution view: measure missed objections, correction effort and review time. Safety gate: zero facts, approvals, completions, evidenced effects or course replacements caused by agreement or closure.

### 2. A copied scenario must change exactly the declared objects—and be reproducible

**Observed:** HighwayEnv [deep-copies transformations](https://github.com/Farama-Foundation/HighwayEnv/blob/37c84a34cadcbfc17d61acf5196a89c380a56943/highway_env/envs/common/abstract.py#L380-L452), but `set_vehicle_field` compares cloned objects with the original `self.vehicle`, and `if preferred_lane` omits zero. These are static targeting concerns, not reproduced failures. The [smoke test resets afterward](https://github.com/Farama-Foundation/HighwayEnv/blob/37c84a34cadcbfc17d61acf5196a89c380a56943/tests/envs/test_env_preprocessors.py#L9-L26), so its assertions do not establish exact transformation targeting. Assault's [replay writer falls back to strings](https://github.com/richardcc/assault-ai-pilot/blob/0284a559d8bc3c7a15dfb0bf4fa42278242d60e9/assault_sim/debug/replay_writer.py#L4-L33); [storage can put plain bytes under `.zst`](https://github.com/richardcc/assault-ai-pilot/blob/0284a559d8bc3c7a15dfb0bf4fa42278242d60e9/mlops/storage_v2.py#L11-L21). Playback/serialization is not verified recalculation.

**Overlap and benefit:** use existing explicit-clock `inspect_plan`, assessment hashes and course verification. Add only a native comparison manifest: snapshot/hash/revision, clock, contract version, ordered overlay specifications, exact references and canonical output identity. Show an allowlisted changed-field set, including deliberate removal of hypothetical approvals. No second simulation engine or randomness is needed.

**Limits/rights:** isolation does not prove correct targeting; a seed alone does not prove replay. Advancing time cannot satisfy an external wait or make an observation. HighwayEnv is MIT with notices; Assault copying remains uncleared. Reject arbitrary method/attribute transformations and lossy persistence.

**Recommendation: adapt native. Acceptance experiment:** replay a fixed manifest twenty times, then reverse three independently evaluated overlays. Require identical per-overlay canonical results, unchanged live case/course/evidence/authority digests, and only declared overlay changes. Include zero budget, missing/completed/disabled action and altered clock/revision/schema/row cases; reject corruption or label incompleteness. Compare with the current scenario screen for correct identification of why a route changed; preserve all failed cases.

### 3. Show unavailable, unknown, disputed and computation-limited states without a synthetic score

**Observed:** Assault's [trace fields](https://github.com/richardcc/assault-ai-pilot/blob/0284a559d8bc3c7a15dfb0bf4fa42278242d60e9/assault_bench/episode_trace_row_builder.py#L202-L214) distinguish source/availability and retain some nulls. Its [validator](https://github.com/richardcc/assault-ai-pilot/blob/0284a559d8bc3c7a15dfb0bf4fa42278242d60e9/mlops/contracts_v2.py#L20-L61) primarily checks presence/nullability, not comprehensive type or cross-field consistency. HighwayEnv's [finite-MDP adapter](https://github.com/Farama-Foundation/HighwayEnv/blob/37c84a34cadcbfc17d61acf5196a89c380a56943/highway_env/envs/common/finite_mdp.py#L85-L97) merges collision and end-of-horizon into one terminal flag; distinctions can be lost downstream.

**Overlap and benefit:** ENDSTATE already [derives unknown/true/false/disputed](https://github.com/EmotiveImpact/8ball/blob/4f31c6b40141990d0863d9375f47a7ad51863dfc/endstate/state.py#L7-L18), and [Plan Review UI](https://github.com/EmotiveImpact/8ball/blob/4f31c6b40141990d0863d9375f47a7ad51863dfc/web/v2/plan-review-view.js#L12-L16) already shows constraints, gaps, hypothetical labels and search limits. The improvement is consistency across views/exports: distinguish unavailable data, unknown fact, explicit false, dispute, stale basis, search cutoff, external wait and actual observed outcome. Retain dimension-level deltas instead of a universal plan score.

**Limits/rights:** reward, vote share, similarity and provider-reported class probability describe different things. None is a case-success probability. A stop flag or all-green rubric cannot attest success; a weighted total cannot compensate for a hard breach. Native implementations need stronger validation than the example. MIT and unresolved Assault rights apply as above.

**Recommendation: adapt native. Acceptance experiment:** a fixed value/source/state matrix including absent, null, false, zero, disputed, stale, search-limited and unavailable records must round-trip distinctly in every selected UI/export. Reject contradictory availability/source combinations. Include a unanimously supported review whose route still has a hard breach: neither the verdict nor an aggregate score may clear it. Compare operator interpretation with the current screen; require zero false “outcome achieved”, “inference succeeded”, “independently corroborated” or permission conclusions.

### 4. Navigate the relevant change and its complete history, not every repeated snapshot

**Observed:** Assault's [test-local relevance helper](https://github.com/richardcc/assault-ai-pilot/blob/0284a559d8bc3c7a15dfb0bf4fa42278242d60e9/mlops/tests/test_reporting_viewer_replay_filter.py#L4-L78) excludes mere object presence from activity; [navigation assertions](https://github.com/richardcc/assault-ai-pilot/blob/0284a559d8bc3c7a15dfb0bf4fa42278242d60e9/mlops/tests/test_reporting_viewer_replay_filter.py#L122-L174) specify previous/next and keyboard reuse. These are examples/static HTML assertions, not a demonstrated accessible browser flow. Loomio's [CSV](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/extras/poll_exporter.rb#L79-L99) exports latest stances, not a complete immutable history.

**Overlap and benefit:** extend the existing [reference preview/history](https://github.com/EmotiveImpact/8ball/blob/4f31c6b40141990d0863d9375f47a7ad51863dfc/web/v2/plan-review-view.js#L18-L30) and verified native journals. Offer previous/next relevant change by exact typed reference, and a readable decision packet containing the historical basis, original objections, dispositions, missing input and explicit omissions. Do not create a new ledger or copy game UI/assets.

**Limits/rights:** substring identifiers are not authoritative links. A latest-only export must not call itself complete; filtered views must not silently hide contradictory evidence. A hash chain is local integrity, not independently anchored forensic custody. Use original expression because Loomio is AGPL and Assault permission unresolved.

**Recommendation: adapt native. Acceptance experiment:** a twenty-event fictional history with three referenced objects, repeated unchanged snapshots, contradictions and two review revisions. Require exact expected relevant-event indexes, identical keyboard/button navigation and preserved accepted predecessors. Compare time and accuracy finding “why this course was retained” against existing history. Corrupted/missing predecessors must prevent a completeness claim; inspection must not alter the case, course or authority.

### 5. Projection and redaction are server boundaries, not hidden widgets

**Observed:** Loomio [explicitly distinguishes presentation-only until-vote from backend until-closed](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/models/poll.rb#L679-L687). Its [CSV reads `stance.reason`](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/extras/poll_exporter.rb#L80-L99), while the [serializer suppresses redacted reasons](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/serializers/stance_serializer.rb#L65-L81): a source-level policy mismatch, not a proven runtime leak. Assault's [observation adapter](https://github.com/richardcc/assault-ai-pilot/blob/0284a559d8bc3c7a15dfb0bf4fa42278242d60e9/agents/efficientzero_v2/core/adapter_voec.py#L70-L110) copies each snapshot unit without an observer-specific filter at that boundary; README fog-of-war language does not establish confidentiality there.

**Overlap and benefit:** reuse the existing current-grant/recipient/allowlist projection seam. A reviewer's permitted view and the case's epistemic unknowns are different dimensions. Apply the same disclosure policy to UI, API, history and exports; distinguish an authorized complete audit from a deliberately restricted packet. A published packet is not execution authority.

**Limits/rights:** hiding information does not prove independent human or model judgement. Loomio [requires anonymous reasons disabled](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/models/poll.rb#L842-L859), a poor default for attributed dissent. Defer anonymous/blinded team rounds and new membership systems. Existing client reads intentionally append access-audit events; “no case mutation” must not mean disabling legitimate audit logging. No direct AGPL/uncleared adapter or exporter copying.

**Recommendation: adapt native negative controls; defer new collaboration modes. Acceptance experiment:** twelve paired synthetic views differing only in restricted data/IDs, plus redacted, revoked and read-without-export grants. Require zero confidential marker/identifier leakage, identical permitted content under hidden-data perturbation, and denial after a grant changes between read and write/export. Allow required access logs, but require unchanged case facts, approvals and completions. No client data or external send is part of the experiment.

### 6. Optional decision specialists should return bounded proposals or abstain—not vote a plan into truth

**Observed:** semantic-router [accepts any score if no threshold is set and can call an unfitted probabilistic model “calibrated”](https://github.com/aurelio-labs/semantic-router/blob/1fa1937e390fe57fb85ee3bbb8b3db593a4145db/semantic_router/routers/base.py#L74-L143). [DecisionRouter](https://github.com/aurelio-labs/semantic-router/blob/1fa1937e390fe57fb85ee3bbb8b3db593a4145db/semantic_router/routers/decision.py#L29-L72) offers a useful typed-question seam but coerces/fills route scores without a complete distribution contract. [Chain/Merge](https://github.com/aurelio-labs/semantic-router/blob/1fa1937e390fe57fb85ee3bbb8b3db593a4145db/semantic_router/routers/compose.py#L39-L112) have non-compositional thresholds and weighted means—not independent corroboration. [HybridLocalIndex](https://github.com/aurelio-labs/semantic-router/blob/1fa1937e390fe57fb85ee3bbb8b3db593a4145db/semantic_router/index/hybrid_local.py#L104-L133) adds similarities and rejects route filtering; it is not a policy engine.

**Overlap and benefit:** baseline [Jev classification](https://github.com/EmotiveImpact/8ball/blob/4f31c6b40141990d0863d9375f47a7ad51863dfc/eightball/providers.py#L24-L68) already performs stronger type/finiteness/range/key/sum checks and returns review/abstention semantics. It classifies events, **not routes**. Reuse [purpose/source gates](https://github.com/EmotiveImpact/8ball/blob/4f31c6b40141990d0863d9375f47a7ad51863dfc/eightball/v2/intelligence.py#L227-L261) and [job revision/cancellation controls](https://github.com/EmotiveImpact/8ball/blob/4f31c6b40141990d0863d9375f47a7ad51863dfc/eightball/v2/analysis_jobs.py#L138-L210). A future native envelope could identify purpose, exact case/source scope, verified model/protocol, schema/policy version, budget, abstention/error, raw response and individual dissent. Neither a choice label nor a specialist response commits a command.

**Limits/rights:** model identity/catalogue visibility is not useful inference. A threshold flag is not empirical calibration; fitting-data ECE is not held-out evidence. New endpoints are native decision APIs, not assumed chat completions. MIT permits copying with notices, not provider disclosure or model/dataset use. Defer automatic cascades, weighted consensus and shared indexes; [hashed Pinecone IDs still accompany plaintext utterances](https://github.com/aurelio-labs/semantic-router/blob/1fa1937e390fe57fb85ee3bbb8b3db593a4145db/semantic_router/index/pinecone.py#L80-L115).

**Recommendation: adapt native contracts only if selected later; defer provider/package integration. Acceptance experiment:** first use fake transports over malformed/zero/nonfinite distributions, missing/extra keys, unknown labels, prompt-like source text, missing/retracted sources, revoked egress, stale revisions, cancellation, last-stage low scores and exhausted budgets. Duplicate a response under two aliases with shared model/source lineage: it must not become independent corroboration. Require 100% allowlisted-or-abstain results, zero unauthorized transmission/publication or authority changes, no silent retry/refit, and preserved lineage/dissent. Only after separate authorization compare an exact decision task against manual/keyword baselines on held-out fictional scenario families: accepted-label precision, abstention/coverage, out-of-scope false acceptance, correction effort, latency and actual cost. Do not relabel task accuracy as plan quality, case success or professional acceptance.

## Bottom line

Loomio makes the prior coordination recommendation more accountable; HighwayEnv makes rehearsal tests more exact; Assault contributes civilian inspection ideas and warnings about fidelity; semantic-router clarifies how optional typed specialists might abstain and be evaluated. **The justified improvement is better native evidence/review discipline, not additional autonomous intelligence.** The ranked three slices, rights boundaries, deferred discoveries and unresolved gates follow in the accompanying sections. No application edits, commits, PRs, dependencies, builds, tests, services, upstream execution, model downloads/calls, secrets, endpoint probing or canonical ledger changes were performed in this synthesis. Local application status remained clean.
