# Loomio → 8BALL/ENDSTATE: source-mining findings

## Verdict and evidence boundary
**Adapt the deliberation semantics natively; do not import Loomio as an application component at this stage.** Its most useful contribution is an attributed reason/objection followed by an explicitly authored disposition, not a voting algorithm. Consensus is a recorded social state, never truth, a success probability, an action approval or proof of effects.

All implementation statements below are observations of pinned source, not runtime results. Loomio's [README](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/README.md#L1-L7) claims a searchable record of discussions, reasons and outcomes; that claim is not evidence of immutable custody, decision quality or suitability for confidential fixer work.

**Existing baseline, not missing capabilities:** at 8BALL commit `4f31c6b40141990d0863d9375f47a7ad51863dfc`, [Plan Review contracts](https://github.com/EmotiveImpact/8ball/blob/4f31c6b40141990d0863d9375f47a7ad51863dfc/eightball/v2/plan_review.py#L19-L54) already require six reasoned judgements, inspected references for supported answers and at most three scenarios. [Assessment/export](https://github.com/EmotiveImpact/8ball/blob/4f31c6b40141990d0863d9375f47a7ad51863dfc/eightball/v2/plan_review.py#L68-L103) binds case revision, full case hash and assessment hash; [save](https://github.com/EmotiveImpact/8ball/blob/4f31c6b40141990d0863d9375f47a7ad51863dfc/eightball/v2/plan_review.py#L137-L179) checks freshness, sequence and idempotency and explicitly records `execution_authorised=False` and `facts_attested=False`. [simulate](https://github.com/EmotiveImpact/8ball/blob/4f31c6b40141990d0863d9375f47a7ad51863dfc/eightball/v2/services.py#L32-L54) is isolated and non-persisting; [Insights](https://github.com/EmotiveImpact/8ball/blob/4f31c6b40141990d0863d9375f47a7ad51863dfc/eightball/v2/insights.py#L244-L274) already separates human review and hypotheses; [Chosen Course](https://github.com/EmotiveImpact/8ball/blob/4f31c6b40141990d0863d9375f47a7ad51863dfc/eightball/v2/courses.py#L221-L263) already retains reasoned, hash-linked, non-authorising selection. These are the natural integration seams. The parent's local Assumption Challenge Brief is not part of this inspected baseline.

## 1. Preserve distinct positions with reasons, not a single agreement score
**Observed source:** [Consent and consensus templates](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/config/poll_templates.yml#L236-L301) distinguish consent/objection from agree/abstain/disagree/block. [Reason validation](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/models/stance.rb#L245-L273) can require reasons specifically for disagreement/block; [Reason input](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/vue/src/components/poll/common/stance_reason.vue#L8-L38) selects a per-option prompt. Stances carry participant identity, poll association and versioned reasons: [stance model](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/models/stance.rb#L65-L88).

**Existing overlap:** Plan Review already has supported/needs_changes/uncertain/not_applicable with meaningful rationales and references. Insights already supports operator-authored hypotheses. Do not add a competing evidence-state system.

**Transferable benefit (hypothesis):** add a bounded objection/contribution view attached to an exact assumption, review criterion or course reference. Preserve the contributor's original wording, an explicit question or proposed remedy, and a separate operator disposition. Distinguish uncertainty about a claim from disagreement with a proposed course and from no response. A majority summary must not replace the minority text.

**Risks/licensing:** Loomio validates nontrivial text, not its substantiation; its reasons are not typed evidence attestations. “Block” is a deliberation label, not proof that a speaker has veto authority. Fictional/archetype perspectives must remain operator-declared hypothetical viewpoints, not simulated humans or corroborating witnesses. Adapt the idea with original native contracts/copy; do not transplant AGPL Ruby/Vue/template expression.

**Recommendation:** **adapt native**, extending existing review/Insights presentation rather than replacing it.
**Proposed acceptance experiment (not run):** on one fictional case, record support, uncertainty, objection and nonresponse; show and export all four distinctly. Dismissing the objection must append a reason without removing its original content. Assert unchanged live case hash, observations, approvals and completion records.

## 2. Separate tally, closing, authored disposition and actual outcome
**Observed source:** [Outcome fields/history](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/models/outcome.rb#L57-L81) has a separate author, statement, optional poll option, latest flag and review date. [Outcome creation](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/services/outcome_service.rb#L31-L79) explicitly creates an authored record and marks older outcomes non-latest. [Outcome authority](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/models/ability/outcome.rb#L9-L16) permits specified people to create/update only when the poll is not active; it does not require a favourable tally. [Closing work](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/services/poll_service.rb#L481-L519) closes the poll and handles results/timeline work, not evidence of real-world success. [Outcome panel](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/vue/src/components/poll/common/outcome_panel.vue#L44-L60) displays author, statement and review due date.

**Existing overlap:** Chosen Course already records rationale, assumptions, replacement acknowledgement and review events; Plan Review already says review recorded is not plan approved. Reuse these distinctions.

**Transferable benefit (hypothesis):** a human disposition can explicitly state “proceed with unresolved objection”, “revise”, “ask for evidence” or “defer”, linking the unresolved contribution IDs and reasons. Keep the case's desired/evidenced outcome vocabulary separate from a deliberation's conclusion. A closure can remain historically visible without claiming that objections disappeared.

**Risks/licensing:** Loomio's `!active?` gate is not a domain-specific mandate or an action-approval gate. Its outcome service also invokes invitations/notifications: do not carry those side effects into a read-only local brief. A review due date is not a background follow-up service. Backend/UI code reuse remains AGPL-sensitive.

**Recommendation:** **adapt native** as explicit disposition/rationale links to existing reviews/courses; **reject** any tally-to-approval or conclusion-to-effect shortcut.
**Proposed acceptance experiment (not run):** close a fictional review containing an unresolved objection and record a conditional course rationale. The objection must remain visible, the conclusion must identify its human author and basis, and no action may become approved/completed or intended effect evidenced. A due review date may show a warning but must send nothing.

## 3. Borrow visible change history, not Loomio's mutable-current-row semantics
**Observed source:** [Stance versioning/latest scopes](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/models/stance.rb#L71-L88) records selected fields through PaperTrail. [Stance update](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/services/stance_service.rb#L38-L83) creates a replacement only when option scores change and either comments exist or the previous update is older than 15 minutes; otherwise it updates the current stance. [Replacement construction](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/models/stance.rb#L129-L137) creates a new stance but does not itself establish a predecessor-hash chain. [Poll version fields](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/models/poll.rb#L252-L270) tracks selected poll fields; [Poll editing](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/services/poll_service.rb#L75-L104) mutates the poll under a lock. [Version retrieval](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/controllers/api/v1/versions_controller.rb#L1-L29) exposes authorised version lookup. These are useful history mechanisms, not the same guarantee as a case-bound immutable derived journal.

**Existing overlap:** 8BALL's review and course journals already bind full snapshots, expected sequences, explicit replacement and previous hashes. They are a stronger fit than replacing them with “latest” rows.

**Transferable benefit (hypothesis):** display a position's evolution and who changed it, but append every accepted substantive change natively with predecessor, case/assessment hash and rationale. Keep history distinct from the current projection. A case/proposal revision should make an old stance historical rather than silently count it as support for new content.

**Risks/licensing:** PaperTrail history is not independently anchored forensic custody; neither is 8BALL's local hash chain. This inspection did not establish a complete Loomio staleness/re-attestation mechanism equivalent to ENDSTATE's exact case binding. Do not copy the AGPL service implementation or the 15-minute replacement heuristic.

**Recommendation:** **adapt native** history presentation; **reject** adopting mutable rows as the authoritative brief/review record.
**Proposed acceptance experiment (not run):** change a reason twice inside 15 minutes, change the case revision, then attempt an old submission. Require all accepted predecessors to remain exportable, reject stale submission, make identical retry idempotent, and detect a tampered journal link.

## 4. Keep participation, access rights and execution authority separate
**Observed source:** [Membership](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/models/membership.rb#L19-L39) distinguishes active/pending/accepted/revoked membership and tracks selected changes. [Vote/export permissions](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/models/ability/poll.rb#L5-L27) requires login, non-deactivation, active poll, visibility, current group participation and an eligible voter record. [Stance rights](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/models/ability/stance.rb#L9-L42) separately gates editing one's latest stance, changing vote weight and redaction. [Locked update](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/services/stance_service.rb#L59-L68) reloads and rechecks authority under a topic lock. [Member removal](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/services/poll_service.rb#L462-L471) revokes relevant active-poll stances, optionally preserving cast votes.

**Existing overlap:** 8BALL already has [matter/object-scoped grants with separate READ/WRITE/EXPORT/INFER operations](https://github.com/EmotiveImpact/8ball/blob/4f31c6b40141990d0863d9375f47a7ad51863dfc/eightball/authorisation.py#L1-L69) and [transactional current-authority rechecking](https://github.com/EmotiveImpact/8ball/blob/4f31c6b40141990d0863d9375f47a7ad51863dfc/eightball/agency_access.py#L101-L107). These controls must not be replaced by Loomio membership. Existing local review/course records still identify `local-operator`; that is not evidence of a verified team deliberation workflow.

**Transferable benefit (hypothesis):** if actual human collaboration is later authorised, model who was invited, who could contribute, who responded, whose grant later expired, and who can publish a disposition independently. Revocation can prevent new actions without erasing an attributed historical statement.

**Risks/licensing:** a group admin is not necessarily a client's authorised approver or qualified specialist. A fictional perspective is not an authenticated member. No production identity or agency acceptance follows from this code inspection. Importing Loomio's Rails/CanCanCan membership stack creates both integration and AGPL obligations.

**Recommendation:** **adapt native** only within existing scoped-policy work; **defer** a new team-election/membership subsystem.
**Proposed acceptance experiment (not run):** in a future authorised team fixture, revoke a contributor's grant between read and write, and give another identity READ without WRITE or EXPORT. Deny every unauthorised write/export, retain historical contributions with revocation status, and leave domain action approvals unchanged.

## 5. Make participation denominators explicit; never convert them into confidence
**Observed source:** [Result calculation](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/services/poll_service.rb#L552-L601) separately computes score share and electorate share and evaluates option thresholds. [Quorum/weight calculations](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/models/poll.rb#L620-L676) distinguishes eligible voters or issued vote weight from votes cast. [Latest/decided/undecided scopes](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/models/stance.rb#L80-L91) separates no response from a cast response. Important edge: result calculation defines score percent as zero when total score is zero, so the consent template's “objections <= 0%” condition can be true with no votes; this is a threshold calculation, not evidence of human consent.

**Existing overlap:** ENDSTATE already keeps unknown conditions and explicit scenario assumptions; Plan Review intentionally has no quality score. Extend that clarity to any contribution summary rather than adding a numeric “confidence” overlay.

**Transferable benefit (hypothesis):** display counts such as “2 of 4 invited humans responded; 1 unresolved objection”, with exact population and time/revision. If only operator-authored hypothetical perspectives exist, label them contributions/viewpoints, not people surveyed. Distinguish “no objection recorded” from “consent recorded”.

**Risks/licensing:** vote weights represent governance choices, not expertise reliability or probability. Several model outputs may share one model/source lineage and are not independent corroboration. Neither a zero-objection threshold nor an all-green rubric can attest truth. The AGPL tally code offers little native value; do not port it.

**Recommendation:** **adapt native** disclosure of participation/missing input; **reject** weighted model voting, confidence percentages and majority-based fact/permission updates.
**Proposed acceptance experiment (not run):** use zero responses, one response out of four, unanimous hypothetical perspectives and a majority with one objection. Every view/export must retain nonresponse and dissent, label hypothetical origin, emit no success probability, and never represent a zero-response review as consent or approval.

## 6. Independent-input rounds require a real server boundary; anonymity conflicts with attributed reasons
**Observed source:** [Result availability/visibility](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/models/poll.rb#L679-L687) explicitly says “until_vote” may send results to the client and is presentation-controlled, whereas “until_closed” is backend-enforced. [Visibility/anonymity invariants](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/models/poll.rb#L823-L859) prevents early result revelation/reopening in the relevant mode and requires anonymous polls to disable reasons. [Anonymous participation masking](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/models/poll.rb#L553-L601) separates masked voter relations from intentional unmasked access and limits participation visibility. [Export/electorate visibility](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/models/ability/poll.rb#L23-L34) authorises exports based on result availability.

**Existing overlap:** current Plan Review records exact snapshots but does not claim an independently blinded panel. The parent's bounded local hypothetical viewpoints are not anonymous or independent human participants.

**Transferable benefit (hypothesis):** a future separately authorised human-review round could collect reasons before revealing others' positions, reducing exposure to prior answers. It must remain distinct from evidence independence and preserve a reviewable identity/provenance policy.

**Risks/licensing:** hiding a chart is not confidentiality; CSV/API/notifications/history must obey the same policy. Anonymous ballots without reasons are a poor fit for the requested retained-dissent brief. Small-group anonymity cannot be inferred from a threshold alone. Do not copy Loomio's AGPL UI or treat client-side until-vote as a security gate.

**Recommendation:** **defer** anonymous/blind team rounds; **reject** presentation-only hiding as access control. Preserve attributed local reasons now.
**Proposed acceptance experiment (not run):** if a sealed-round design is later authorised, place a unique marker in a withheld reason and inspect every permitted read surface before reveal (API, export, history and notification payloads). Require zero marker disclosure, then require one explicit reveal event without retroactively changing any contribution.

## 7. Export is a separate audit/privacy product, not just a convenient CSV
**Observed source:** [CSV exporter](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/extras/poll_exporter.rb#L42-L99) provides poll metadata, option results, current outcome, voter identifiers, membership title/delegate/weight, times and reasons. It exports `stances.latest`, not full stance/outcome version history. [Controller](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/controllers/polls_controller.rb#L8-L21) authorises the export before sending CSV/HTML/BLT. It is useful review material, not a self-contained immutable audit package.

**Concrete source-level discrepancy:** CSV rows directly read `stance.reason` at line 93; the [latest scope](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/models/stance.rb#L80-L89) excludes revoked records but not redacted ones. By contrast, [API serializer](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/serializers/stance_serializer.rb#L65-L81) suppresses redacted reasons and related attachments/previews. No redaction guard appears in the inspected CSV path. **This is a source-observed policy mismatch, not a runtime-confirmed exposure or a complete security audit.**

**Existing overlap:** 8BALL already verifies case-linked, predecessor-linked [review exports](https://github.com/EmotiveImpact/8ball/blob/4f31c6b40141990d0863d9375f47a7ad51863dfc/eightball/v2/plan_review.py#L83-L103) and stores non-authorising course records. Preserve old source-free export compatibility; avoid replacing these with a current-state spreadsheet.

**Transferable benefit (hypothesis):** offer a readable decision packet derived from the verified native journal: exact snapshot/version, original contributions, all dispositions and predecessors, missing participants, unresolved objections and authority/evidence disclaimers. Maintain an authorised complete audit and an explicitly labelled audience projection; neither should masquerade as the other.

**Risks/licensing:** raw exports can disclose names, internal allegations, reasons and retired material. An export-only projection must apply explicit permissions, including redaction/omission policy, not reuse UI assumptions. Do not transplant the AGPL exporter; native data exports are not automatically AGPL, but copied template/expression and privacy rules require separate analysis.

**Recommendation:** **adapt native** a loss-aware export projection; **reject** latest-only CSV as authoritative history and direct reuse of the inspected exporter.
**Proposed acceptance experiment (not run):** create two revisions, a replacement, a revoked contribution and a redacted confidential marker. An authorised full audit must verify permitted history and hashes; a restricted packet must omit the marker across all formats and disclose omissions without leaking their content. Any claimed complete packet missing an accepted predecessor must fail completeness validation.

## Integration recommendations

## Bounded implementation direction for the parent (recommendations, not accepted scope)
1. **Highest-value native candidate:** augment the existing review/course experience with original, attributed reason/objection records and an explicit human disposition linking unresolved objections. Reuse Plan Review/Insights/Chosen Course provenance and concurrency instead of installing a deliberation platform. A possible future contract could identify case/revision/hash, target reference, contribution kind, author/provenance, text, predecessor and disposition; those names are proposed semantics, not existing code paths.
2. **Use current infrastructure first:** [review save](https://github.com/EmotiveImpact/8ball/blob/4f31c6b40141990d0863d9375f47a7ad51863dfc/eightball/v2/plan_review.py#L137-L179) already provides freshness, sequence, idempotency and no-authority boundaries; [course selection](https://github.com/EmotiveImpact/8ball/blob/4f31c6b40141990d0863d9375f47a7ad51863dfc/eightball/v2/courses.py#L232-L263) already requires explicit replacement and exact preview binding. [Explicit Insight question creation](https://github.com/EmotiveImpact/8ball/blob/4f31c6b40141990d0863d9375f47a7ad51863dfc/eightball/v2/insights.py#L300-L327) already demonstrates how a derived finding can lead to a separately reviewed case command. Do not describe inspection, simulation, Insights, questions or Chosen Course as missing.
3. **Keep product/core ownership clear:** fixer wording, local authorship, review UI, grants, storage and export projections belong in 8BALL. Any genuinely reusable validation of detached typed contribution/disposition records may belong at the ENDSTATE contract boundary only if it has another demonstrated use. No Rails/Vue dependency, second truth store, separate app or new agent runtime is justified by this inspection.
4. **Fit the parent's unvalidated local brief without inventing implementation:** retain the supplied bounds of at most four operator-declared fictional/archetype perspectives, twelve contributions and three explicit scenario overlays. Treat these as scope constraints supplied by the parent, not inspected source features. Link every hypothetical contribution to the exact live case revision/hash; retain dissent and immutable predecessor history. No simulated named people, psychological profiles, inferred permissions, independent-corroboration claims or model-vote percentages.
5. **No automatic escalation of meaning:** importing a contribution is not accepting it; accepting it as useful is not attesting its claim; closing a review is not choosing a course; choosing a course is not approving an action; completing an action is not evidence of its effect. A shared-model agreement is not independent corroboration. Human disposition remains explicit. Preserve existing conservative approval invalidation.
6. **Adoption order:** first evaluate reason/disposition usefulness on a fictional case using the proposed experiments in findings 1–3 and 7; preserve counterexamples. Denominator disclosures are safeguards, not a voting feature. Defer blind/anonymous human rounds and external Loomio deployment until a real collaborative need, identity/publication policy and licensing review justify them. Reject weighted model voting and importing automatic invitation/notification behaviour.
7. **Measure value, not popularity:** proposed owner evaluation should compare ability to locate unresolved objections and their provenance, missed dissent, incorrect permission/fact inferences, and added operator review time against existing Plan Review/Chosen Course. Source inspection alone supplies no measured improvement or professional/model/production acceptance. Parent owns any task/decision/register updates; none were made here.

## Discoveries to preserve, including deferred/rejected ideas
These are source-inspection findings, not build acceptance and not assigned canonical EM IDs. Suggested dispositions are for the parent to decide.

- **Existing_task / design clarification — review completion needs a dissent-preserving disposition.** Loomio's separate authored Outcome shows a useful distinction between aggregating input and explaining a conclusion ([source](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/services/outcome_service.rb#L31-L79)); 8BALL already has the non-authorising review/course foundation. Proposed incremental value is explicit unresolved-objection linkage, not another planner.
- **Decision_record candidate — anonymous consultation and accountable reasons are different products.** Loomio forces anonymous reasons off ([source](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/models/poll.rb#L842-L859)). For a bounded fixer brief, provenance and retained objections are the priority; anonymous ballots are deferred rather than assumed universally safer. No confidential-person or psychological simulation should fill the gap.
- **No_action / reject — a passed numeric rule can be empty of human assent.** Zero score yields zero percent while consent tests objections <= 0 ([calculation](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/services/poll_service.rb#L552-L574); [template](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/config/poll_templates.yml#L236-L259)). This is not a demonstrated Loomio decision defect; it is a reason never to interpret a numeric poll requirement as evidence, consent, authority or probability in ENDSTATE.
- **Existing_task / safeguard — a current projection and an immutable historical basis must remain distinct.** Loomio's short-window in-place edits and latest-only export are useful application choices but unsuitable as replacements for current 8BALL case-bound append-only journals ([update](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/services/stance_service.rb#L38-L83); [export](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/extras/poll_exporter.rb#L79-L99)). Preserve predecessor-bound brief history rather than downgrading it.
- **New_candidate / privacy regression — export can contradict ordinary read redaction.** The inspected CSV path accesses a redacted reason that the API serializer would omit ([CSV](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/extras/poll_exporter.rb#L80-L99); [serializer](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/serializers/stance_serializer.rb#L65-L81)). Runtime impact remains untested. Do not copy the exporter; use the finding to specify projection/redaction parity tests, including retained history.
- **Decision_record candidate / defer — independence is not a hidden-results switch.** Until-vote is client presentation whereas until-closed is server availability ([source](https://github.com/loomio/loomio/blob/24380e2131c1e43594a241814f3f460a9b0065f6/app/models/poll.rb#L679-L687)). Even a correctly sealed human round would reduce exposure to previous responses, not establish independent evidence or independent model reasoning.
- **No_action on stack adoption — source availability is not a component fit.** Rails/Postgres/PaperTrail plus Vue and AGPL/network/conveyance responsibilities are material costs for a Python/local-first system that already has deterministic review/history primitives. Preserve Loomio as research; no runtime component is currently recommended. Separate hosting alone does not remove those obligations.
