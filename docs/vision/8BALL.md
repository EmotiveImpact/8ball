# 8BALL vision: a live situation room for the fixer

Revision 1.0, 24 September 2026. **Design direction, not a new runtime release.**

## The proposition

**8BALL helps a fixer understand the situation, agree what a good resolution means, choose a defensible course and keep that course under review as reality changes. Powered by ENDSTATE.**

We sell a useful professional workflow first, not a promise that AI can control people or solve any problem. The product remains 8BALL for fixers and situation-handling agencies. Sales, customer service and other applications remain future uses of the same engine, not parallel launch obligations.

## What the operator should be able to answer

What do we know, and from where? What are we trying to achieve, and what must we not compromise? Which course have we chosen, and why? What needs doing or finding out now? What would make us reconsider? What would count as an evidenced resolution?

The existing case, source, graph, plan-review and insight tools provide parts of this experience. A complete client mandate, selected-course record, durable reconsideration service and outcome-stability workflow are **not** implemented by this document.

## 1. Agree the mandate before optimising the route

A case should eventually distinguish the client's requested outcome from the constraints and authority under which the fixer may act. Ask for the ideal result, the minimum acceptable result where relevant, hard prohibitions, who may agree concessions, and what evidence would establish resolution. Missing answers remain open questions, not model-created permissions.

A fallback is a separate, explicitly authorised possibility. The software must never quietly lower the objective because the original one is difficult. Evidence of a participant's preference is not authority to change the client's target. Existing mandatory/optional objectives and failure criteria remain in force; an additional Outcome Contract must earn its place rather than duplicate them.

**Example:** “Keep the customer” is not an adequate mandate on its own. “Obtain a twelve-month renewal within approved commercial terms; do not conceal the incident; acceptance must come from an authorised representative” is a reviewable target and boundary.

## 2. Distinguish options from the chosen course

A route list answers what might be possible. The operator also needs a durable record of which course the team is actually pursuing, who selected it, what they relied on and when it should be reconsidered.

A proposed Course Record names the exact case revision, target revision, route identity and dependencies, selected actions, declared assumptions, alternatives considered and selection rationale. Selecting it grants no authority to execute its actions, creates no factual observations and predicts no success. Different teams need not lose legitimate parallel work, but overlapping commitments must be visible.

Do not add another graph database or separate application just to store this distinction. Begin with a versioned record in the existing situation room and timeline, supported by neutral ENDSTATE calculation inputs.

## 3. Show what a change means for the chosen course

A new source, a reviewed observation, an adopted restriction and an authorised instruction are different events. An unreviewed email cannot rewrite the live plan.

After a committed update, explain whether the chosen course needs review, which dependencies or assumptions changed, and which alternatives remain candidates. Do not automatically switch the team to the top-ranked route. A loss of source access or incomplete computation produces “review required / not established”, not reassurance that nothing changed.

The current conservative approval-invalidation rules remain unchanged. Dependency-specific revalidation may help focus explanations later, but it must not preserve approval through a substantive update unless a separate authority policy and adversarial tests justify that behaviour.

## 4. Make recovery a normal part of the work

A failed model request, rejected proposal, unavailable action or missing fact must have a useful next step: inspect the failure, capture the missing information, correct a separate draft, request review, use an explicitly labelled catalogue, or continue manually.

Human repairs must retain the original output, validation errors, source set, exact edits, responsible reviewer and changed target assumptions. They create a new pending proposal, never a hidden replacement for the failed model result. A human-assisted recovery is a different evaluation outcome from an unassisted model success.

The previous Draft Repair handoff describes this work but says its completed source was not recovered. Treat runtime availability and its test totals as unverified for the recovered alpha.7 source until the actual implementation is restored and reproduced.

## 5. Make the limits of the operating picture visible

The source archive is not the same as the excerpts selected for analysis. Exact source matching is not semantic understanding. A bounded search over supplied actions is not a search of every action a fixer could take.

Bring these distinctions into the existing workspace: originals available, selected passages, unprocessed or rejected material, known source relationships, unresolved references, source/review times and search limits. Avoid a single “case confidence” or “coverage percentage” that confuses these different quantities.

A helpful negative result is: “No route was found within this catalogue and these assumptions; these three unknowns could change the result.” It is not: “There is no way through.”

## 6. Record obligations explicitly, then verify the actual resolution

A promise to deliver an interim report can remain operationally important even if the message supporting its interpretation is later withdrawn. Source reliability, an adopted deadline and an authorised commitment need separate histories. The current reviewed-deadline behaviour is a bounded example, not a complete commitments system.

Likewise, where the mandate requires sustained recovery, one successful check does not establish that the condition held for the required period. Record the required time window and confirming authority explicitly; do not invent universal durability periods or silently extend case monitoring.

## 7. Measure usefulness without inflating the claims

Test whether a fixer reaches a sound next decision faster, misses fewer important dependencies, understands the evidence, can recover from errors and can explain their selected course. Measure false alarms, missed insights, correction burden and avoidable repeated reviews as well as speed. Use representative cases and genuinely human judgement, not just developer-written fixtures.

A larger graph, more findings, more pages and a higher test count are not, by themselves, customer value. Keep the black interface focused: current course, immediate work, critical questions, decisions and reasons to reconsider. Put detailed graphs and provenance behind deliberate inspection. Do not create another top-level screen for every new concept.

## Product boundary

Continue to require factual evidence, explicit human authority, protected client data and reviewable history. No automatic external communications, paid actions, coercion, concealment or unapproved cross-client learning follows from this vision. Confidential agency use remains gated by V0.3 security and professional pilot requirements.
